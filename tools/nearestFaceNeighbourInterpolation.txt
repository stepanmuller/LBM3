u_x_children = M_x * S; u_y_children = M_y * S; u_z_children = M_z * S.
Each M is 8 x 63. EVERY INTEGER PRINTED BELOW MUST BE MULTIPLIED BY 1/64.

Nx,Ny,Nz use coarse-spacing-normalized coordinates.
Dxy,Dxz,Dyz are derivative SUMS, not half-sums.
